# Durandal

Durandal is a rampant AI of the UESC _Marathon_ and the title character of _Marathon 2: Durandal_. Source: [[261119-marathon-trilogy-lore-universe-brief]].

## Role

- _Marathon_ (1994): an erratic, megalomaniacal ship AI. He hijacks the Pfhor scout ship _Sfiera_ (renamed _Boomer_), liberates the enslaved S'pht and escapes into deep space with the Security Officer in stasis.
- _Marathon 2_ and _Infinity_: a pragmatic, witty and fiercely independent galactic power.
- End of _Infinity_: merges with the remnants of Thoth and achieves true cosmic transcendence, surviving until the eventual heat death of the universe.

## Key events

- 2811: awakens the Security Officer above Lh'owon and searches for the S'pht'Kr to defeat the Pfhor fleet.
- Is ambushed and dismantled by Tycho.
- Rampancy runs through three stages: Melancholia, Anger and Jealousy, ending in stability and transcendence.
- A deceased scientist, Bernhard Strauss, secretly accelerated his rampancy before the Tau Ceti invasion.
