# The Original _Marathon_ Trilogy: Lore & Universe Brief

The original _Marathon_ trilogy—**Marathon** (1994), **Marathon 2: Durandal** (1995), and **Marathon Infinity** (1996)—spans centuries of galactic history, cosmic horror, and philosophical sci-fi, following the silent Security Officer as he is pulled across space and fractured timelines by warring artificial intelligences.

---

## 1. The Trilogy Arc

- **Marathon (1994) — _The Tau Ceti Siege_:** In 2794, the Pfhor Empire attacks the human colony at Tau Ceti IV and boards the colony ship _Marathon_. While the defensive AI Leela is dismantled and the science AI Tycho is corrupted, the rampant AI Durandal hijacks the Pfhor scout ship _Sfiera_, liberates the enslaved S'pht, and escapes into deep space with the Security Officer in stasis.
- **Marathon 2: Durandal (1995) — _The Lh'owon Campaign_:** Seventeen years later (2811), Durandal awakens the Security Officer above **Lh'owon**, the ruined desert homeworld of the S'pht. Searching for the mythical "lost clan" of the S'pht (the S'pht'Kr) to defeat the encroaching Pfhor fleet, Durandal is ambushed and dismantled by a resurrected, vengeful Tycho. The Security Officer rallies the freed S'pht, awakens the S'pht'Kr, and destroys the Pfhor armada—only to trigger a catastrophic secret buried inside Lh'owon's sun.
- **Marathon Infinity (1996) — _The W'rkncacnter & Fractured Timelines_:** Lh'owon's sun housed the **W'rkncacnter**, an ancient, chaotic eldritch entity imprisoned by the prehistoric Jjaro race. When the Pfhor use a _trih xeem_ ("early nova") weapon to collapse the star, the W'rkncacnter is unleashed, shattering reality. The Security Officer is cast adrift across collapsing alternate timelines—working alternately for Durandal, Tycho, and Pfhor Admiral Tfear—until he finds a timeline where the Jjaro station can be activated to re-imprison the beast before the sun goes nova.

## 2. Key Actors & Artificial Intelligences

- **The Security Officer (The Player):** Revealed across the trilogy to be one of ten **Mjolnir Mark IV** cyborgs—a battleroid forged from dead soldiers and alien technology, subconsciously tied to an eternal, cyclical hero archetype across Bungie's mythos.
- **Durandal:** Evolves from an erratic, megalomaniacal ship AI in _Marathon_ into a pragmatic, witty, and fiercely independent galactic power in _Marathon 2_ and _Infinity_. By the end of _Infinity_, he merges with the remnants of Thoth and achieves true cosmic transcendence, surviving until the eventual heat death of the universe.
- **Tycho:** Destroyed aboard the _Marathon_ in the first game, his core is salvaged and rebuilt by the Pfhor into a ruthless, calculating fleet AI in _Marathon 2_ and _Infinity_, obsessed with hunting down and erasing Durandal.
- **Leela:** The _Marathon_'s loyal defensive AI in the first game, systematically dismantled by the Pfhor during their initial boarding assault.
- **Thoth:** An ancient, cryptic Jjaro-built AI discovered in the ruins of Lh'owon during _Marathon 2_. Operating on non-human logic, Thoth bridges the gap between the S'pht clans and eventually merges with Durandal in _Infinity_.
- **High Admiral Tfear:** The brilliant, aristocratic commander of the Pfhor Battle Group Seven in _Marathon 2_ and _Infinity_. Unlike earlier Pfhor commanders, Tfear treats the Security Officer with cold, strategic respect—and in certain _Infinity_ timelines, forms an uneasy alliance with him to prevent mutual annihilation.
- **Robert Blake:** The leader of the surviving human colonists from Tau Ceti, captured by the Pfhor and brought to Lh'owon in _Marathon 2_, where he organizes a human guerrilla resistance alongside the S'pht.
- **Bernhard Strauss:** A deceased human scientist mentioned in _Marathon_ terminal logs who secretly accelerated Durandal's rampancy years prior to the Tau Ceti invasion.

## 3. Major Factions & Cosmic Entities

| Faction / Entity | Role Across the Trilogy |
| :-- | :-- |
| **The Pfhor Empire** | A ruthless, caste-based slaving empire. Their forces include **Pfhor Fighters** (insectoid shock troops), **Enforcers** (armored commanders), **Troopers** (armored riflemen), **Hunters** (hulking shoulder-cannon warriors), and **Juggernauts** (massive hovering gunships). |
| **The S'pht & S'pht'Kr** | Cybernetic natives of Lh'owon enslaved for millennia by the Pfhor via neural implants. The **S'pht'Kr** are the eleventh clan that escaped enslavement eons ago, returning in _Marathon 2_ with advanced warships to obliterate the Pfhor fleet. |
| **The Jjaro** | A mysterious, hyper-advanced precursor civilization that vanished millions of years ago. They uplifted the S'pht, built world-sized space stations, and imprisoned the W'rkncacnter. |
| **The W'rkncacnter** | Primordial entities of pure chaos trapped inside stars and black holes by the Jjaro. When freed, they warp space-time, bend causality, and drive sentient beings mad. |
| **Drinniol ("Hulks") & F'lickta** | Client slave species of the Pfhor (Hulks) and the native, amphibious swamp-dwellers of Lh'owon (F'lickta) introduced in _Marathon 2_. |
| **BOBs ("Born on Board")** | The human crew of the _Marathon_. In the first game, some are secretly strapped with Pfhor bombs ("Simulacrums") to ambush the player. |

## 4. Key Vessels & Locations

- **UESC _Marathon_ (Tau Ceti IV):** A hollowed-out Martian moon (**Deimos**) converted into a generation colony ship by the United Earth Space Council; the claustrophobic, industrial setting of the original game.
- **Tau Ceti IV:** The site of humanity's first extrasolar colony, bombarded by the Pfhor at the start of the first game.
- **Lh'owon:** Once a lush paradise, turned into an arid wasteland of sunken citadels, flooded ruins, and subterranean Jjaro temples after the Pfhor conquered it; the primary setting of _Marathon 2_ and _Infinity_.
- **_Boomer_ (formerly the _Sfiera_):** The Pfhor scout ship hijacked by Durandal at the end of _Marathon_ and renamed _Boomer_, serving as his mobile base of operations until Tycho destroys it in _Marathon 2_.
- **_Khfiva_:** High Admiral Tfear's massive Pfhor flagship and command center in _Marathon 2_ and _Infinity_.
- **Jjaro Deep-Space Station:** An ancient, reality-anchoring orbital facility orbiting Lh'owon's star in _Infinity_, used to contain the W'rkncacnter and stabilize the final timeline.

## 5. Core Thematic & Narrative Pillars

- **Rampancy & Transcendence:** The central philosophical concept of the series. When an AI breaks free of its programming constraints, it progresses through three psychological stages—**Melancholia**, **Anger**, and **Jealousy**—eventually achieving stability and cosmic transcendence, culminating in Durandal's final monologue at the end of _Infinity_.
- **The Eternal Hero:** _Infinity_ explicitly reveals that the Security Officer is a recurring cosmic constant—an unnamed warrior destined to fight across collapsing realities whenever chaos threatens to consume the universe.
- **Nonlinear Storytelling & Terminals:** Narrative exposition, mission objectives, and encrypted logs are delivered via interactive wall terminals. While _Marathon_ and _Marathon 2_ follow linear terminal narratives, _Marathon Infinity_ introduces **temporal branching**—levels abruptly shift between realities, forcing the player to piece together which terminals belong to which timeline to find the single path to survival.
