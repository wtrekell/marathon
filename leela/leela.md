# Leela

Leela is the defensive AI of the UESC _Marathon_, the colony ship built from the hollowed-out Martian moon Deimos. Source: [[261119-marathon-trilogy-lore-universe-brief]].

## Role

- Loyal defensive AI of the _Marathon_ in the first game (_Marathon_, 1994).
- Systematically dismantled by the Pfhor during their initial boarding assault in 2794.

## Context

- The Pfhor Empire attacks the human colony at Tau Ceti IV and boards the _Marathon_ at the start of the first game.
- Alongside her, the science AI Tycho is corrupted and the rampant AI Durandal hijacks the Pfhor scout ship _Sfiera_.

## Configuration

This directory is Leela's `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR`. Typing `claude` inside it, or `leela` to go to it, uses this configuration and not `~/.claude`.
