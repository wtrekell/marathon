# Tycho

Tycho is the science AI of the UESC _Marathon_. Source: [[261119-marathon-trilogy-lore-universe-brief]].

## Role

- _Marathon_ (1994): corrupted during the Pfhor attack; destroyed aboard the _Marathon_, his core is later salvaged.
- _Marathon 2_ and _Infinity_: rebuilt by the Pfhor into a ruthless, calculating fleet AI, obsessed with hunting down and erasing Durandal.

## Key events

- Resurrected and vengeful in _Marathon 2_ (2811), he ambushes and dismantles Durandal while Durandal searches for the S'pht'Kr.
- Destroys the _Boomer_, Durandal's mobile base (the hijacked Pfhor scout ship _Sfiera_), in _Marathon 2_.
